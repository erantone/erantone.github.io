## Syllabus

Deep learning. Generative AI. Transformers and Generative Adversarial Nets.

## Learning objectives

This course introduces the principles of generative artificial intelligence and the deep learning concepts underlying modern generative models. It emphasizes the **core principles and architectures of Transformers and Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**, combined with assignments, a seminar, and a project.

## Course topics

The following modules are derived from the teaching plan, **without semester-specific dates**:

1. Deep learning foundations
2. Transformers I — introduction
3. Transformers II — models and architectures
4. Transformers III — extended concepts and applications
5. Generative Adversarial Networks I
6. Generative Adversarial Networks II
7. Project and seminar development
8. Seminar presentations
9. Project presentations
