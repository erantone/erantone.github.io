## Syllabus

Regression, classification, neural networks, reinforcement learning, and unsupervised learning.

## Learning objectives

The course aims to provide a solid understanding of fundamental machine learning concepts and algorithms, emphasizing **core principles, mathematical intuition, and algorithmic foundations**. Students develop familiarity with supervised learning for regression and classification, neural networks, reinforcement learning, and unsupervised methods.

## Course topics

The following is a **topic sequence**, not a semester calendar:

1. Course introduction; machine learning and supervised learning
2. Regression
3. Classification and regularization
4. Neural networks I — foundations
5. Neural networks II — training and modeling
6. Reinforcement learning I
7. Reinforcement learning II; introduction to Convolutional Neural Networks
8. Unsupervised learning and clustering
9. Project presentations and discussion
