## Research interests

- **Autonomous systems:** imitation learning, autonomous driving, robot navigation, and generative policies
- **Physics-informed modeling and control:** neural models of dynamical systems, simulation, identification, and control
- **Recurrent neural networks:** reservoir computing, Echo State Networks, and online learning
- **Applied machine learning:** industrial process modeling, soft sensors, and data-driven control

Current group projects, publications, and demonstrations are featured on the **[GSAI — Intelligent Autonomous Systems Group](https://gsai.ufsc.br/)** website.
