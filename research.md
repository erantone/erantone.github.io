## Autonomous systems and imitation learning {#autonomous-systems}

My work in autonomous systems investigates how learning agents can acquire reliable behavior from experience and demonstrations. Recent directions include **behavior cloning, generative adversarial imitation learning (GAIL), energy-based models, and diffusion-based policies**, particularly for autonomous driving in urban environments.

Projects have explored perception and control using **Bird's-Eye View (BEV)** representations, the CARLA simulator, and miniature autonomous vehicles. Current group activities and demonstrations are documented at [GSAI](https://erantone.github.io/gsai/).

## Physics-informed modeling and control {#physics-informed-control}

An important research direction is integrating prior physical knowledge into machine learning, especially for dynamic systems governed by differential equations. This includes **Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Control (PINC)** and data-driven neural models for efficient simulation, system identification, and control.

Selected publication: [*Physics-Informed Neural Nets for Control of Dynamical Systems* — Neurocomputing (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2024.127419).

## Reservoir computing and recurrent neural networks {#reservoir-computing}

My earlier research has examined **Echo State Networks, reservoir computing, online learning, and recurrent neural network models**. Applications include autonomous robot navigation and localization, efficient modeling of nonlinear systems, and neural control of industrial processes.

Selected publication: [*On Learning Navigation Behaviors for Small Mobile Robots with Reservoir Computing Architectures* — IEEE TNNLS](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNNLS.2014.2323247).

## Industrial applications

Applications of data-driven dynamical modeling include process monitoring, soft sensors, and modeling and control in oil and gas production. These activities complement my methodological research on recurrent and physics-informed machine learning.
